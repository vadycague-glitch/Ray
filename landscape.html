His days off

The Voider lives alone in a dark, quiet apartment where everything is simple and organized.He wakes up late, makes something quick to eat, cleans up a little, and spends most of his day training his body and practicing control over his Void powers. When he is not training, he relaxes by watching anime, listening to music, or playing games. At night, he cooks dinner, takes a shower, and sits near the window enjoying the silence before checking the city for danger. Even though his home feels peaceful, the emptiness sometimes reminds him that he has no one waiting for him.
