cosmic hole

The Void Galaxy is one of the most dangerous places in existence, hidden beyond the edge of the known universe. To reach it, the Voider must use a massive amount of Void energy to tear open a portal through space. Once he enters, the portal immediately closes behind him, leaving no easy way back. The galaxy is filled with collapsing planets, violent energy storms, black stars, and powerful creatures that hunt anything they sense. Even the Voider’s powers become unstable there, meaning one mistake could leave him trapped in the Void Galaxy forever.
