The challenges

The Voider has been sent on many dangerous missions to stop creatures from escaping the Void Galaxy and destroying other worlds. During those missions, he fought beside partners and close friends, but not everyone made it back. Some sacrificed themselves to save him, while others disappeared into the Void and were never seen again. Every loss made him stronger, but also more distant. He began blaming himself and wondering why he was always the one who survived. Now, most of his missions are done alone because he is afraid of losing someone else. Behind all his power is someone carrying memories of the people he could not save, fighting enemies in the darkness while struggling with a loneliness that follows him everywhere.
